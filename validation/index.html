Three OTD modes on the laminar channel flow at Re = 5000. The growth rates are compared with theory: viscous decay and the Orr-Sommerfeld eigenvalue.
3 reference checks
Reference values

The expected values are exact or from theory, not read back from an OTD run. Mode 1 is x-independent and decays by viscosity alone: -nu (pi/2)^2 = -4.934802e-4. Modes 2 and 3 span the Tollmien-Schlichting wave of wavenumber 1, whose Orr-Sommerfeld eigenvalue at Re = 5000 is -0.00175034 +- 0.26813148 i. The eigenvalues in otd_eigenvalues.dat are the diagonal entries of the upper triangular reduced operator, so the pair appears as two real numbers whose mean is the real part sigma. At t = 5000 the run gave -4.935449e-4, -1.751402e-3 and -1.752725e-3: mode 1 is 6.5e-8 from its exact value (the error falls by a factor 12 per 1000 time units) and the pair is 0.06 % and 0.14 % from sigma. The entries of the pair move slowly after t = 5000: in a run to t = 10000 they stay within 1.0 % of sigma and their mean within 0.55 %, so the tolerance of 0.5 % holds at the end time of this stage and is not a statement about other end times.
